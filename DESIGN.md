# PHOENIX Product & Visual Design Language

## Desired feeling

Professional, futuristic, calm, precise, premium, capable.

Avoid: anime-fan-site appearance, excessive neon, game-like UI, clutter, constant agent chatter.

## Principles

- Dark-first professional interface.
- Strong information hierarchy.
- Motion communicates state.
- Voice state is obvious: sleeping, listening, thinking, speaking.
- Permission prompts clearly show risk, scope, and consequence.
- Errors show what failed and what Phoenix will do next.
- Recovery is visible.

## Agent cards

Show both display alias and professional role.

Example:

```text
MAHORAGA
Adaptive Recovery
STANDBY
```

## Audio

- PHOENIX owns primary voice.
- Agent voices are rare and event-driven.
- Startup uses one Phoenix roll-call summary, not 29 spoken lines.
- Fixed signature lines may be cached.
- Dynamic TTS is used when necessary.
