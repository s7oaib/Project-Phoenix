# PHOENIX V1 Roadmap

1. **Foundation** — repo, backend/frontend shells, config, persistence, events, hardware scan.
2. **Model Runtime** — provider abstraction, Ollama, lifecycle, embeddings, Colibri adapter, benchmarks.
3. **Orchestration** — registry, PHOENIX, AIZEN, routing, task graph, LEVI, MAHORAGA, KAIDO.
4. **Memory & RAG** — PUDDING, ingestion, vectors, retrieval, citations.
5. **Tools & Security** — ToolRegistry, permissions, audit, filesystem, terminal, secrets.
6. **Computer & Web** — Playwright, Windows automation, screenshots/vision, URAHARA research.
7. **Voice** — wake word, VAD, STT, TTS, conversation state, barge-in.
8. **Media & Documents** — Spotify, YouTube, uploads, parsing, generation.
9. **Desktop Experience** — chat, agents, tasks, permissions, memory, files, settings, observability.
10. **Validation & Release** — tests, benchmarks, packaging, demos, FYP evaluation.

A phase is complete only when required tickets pass acceptance criteria and invariants remain satisfied.
